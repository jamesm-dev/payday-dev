# Live Bank Sync

## Pitch

Connect your bank, card and loan accounts through an account-aggregation service, so transactions
and balances arrive on their own — no export, no typing.

## Why it might work

- The ambitious end of capture, marked #stretch: if the data arrives by itself, the tracking habit
  stops being the bottleneck at all.
- Balances update too, so [[Safe To Spend Number]] and [[Loan Ledger With Debt Free Date]] stay
  current without a check-in.

## Why it might not

- Aggregators are paid services with their own sign-up and approval, and coverage depends on your
  country and your specific banks and lenders. None of that has been checked.
- An app you built would hold access to your own bank accounts. Security stops being a nice-to-have.
- Connections break, and a broken sync quietly produces stale numbers.

> [!warning] Riskiest assumption
> That an aggregator covers your banks and your loan providers, at a price that makes sense for one
> person.

## What would raise confidence

- [ ] List your banks and lenders, then check one aggregator's coverage and personal-use pricing
  against that list.
