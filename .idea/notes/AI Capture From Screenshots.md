# AI Capture From Screenshots

## Pitch

Screenshot a bank app, photograph a receipt, or paste a text-message alert, and a model turns it into
transactions for you to confirm.

## Why it might work

- The horizon bet, tagged #horizon-bet: vision-capable models keep getting cheaper and more accurate,
  and smaller ones increasingly run on the phone itself. Within 12-24 months, reading any
  money-shaped image or message may be close to free, and private.
- It works with any bank, with no export format and no aggregator.

## Why it might not

- Amounts must be exactly right. A model that is 98% accurate is wrong several times a month.
- Sending screenshots of your finances to a hosted model is a privacy decision to make on purpose,
  not by default.

> [!warning] Riskiest assumption
> That extraction is accurate enough that confirming a parsed transaction is faster than typing it.

## What would raise confidence

- [ ] Give five screenshots of real bank notifications to a current model and check every amount,
  date and merchant it extracts.
