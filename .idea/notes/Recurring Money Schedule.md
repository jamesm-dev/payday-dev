# Recurring Money Schedule

## Pitch

Enter every paycheck, bill and loan payment once, as a repeating rule — every other Friday, the 15th
of each month, every March — and the app knows the whole year in advance.

## Why it might work

- It compounds. [[Safe To Spend Number]], [[Paycheck Period Budgeting]], [[Cash Flow Runway]] and
  [[Loan Ledger With Debt Free Date]] all read the same schedule, so building it once makes each of
  them cheap.
- Setup is a one-time hour, not a daily chore — it fits the constraint that daily discipline has not
  worked.
- Yearly and quarterly items, like insurance and renewals, get a little set aside each paycheck. That
  is what keeps the safe-to-spend number honest.

## Why it might not

- It is invisible on its own. Nobody opens an app to admire a schedule; its value only shows through
  the views built on it.
- Real schedules drift — an amount changes, a bill is skipped, a loan is paid off early — so one-off
  overrides have to be easy.

> [!warning] Riskiest assumption
> That you know your bills and due dates well enough to enter them. If you do not, the first job is
> finding them, probably in your bank statements.

## What would raise confidence

- [ ] List every recurring payment in your last two bank statements and count how many you would
  have forgotten from memory.
