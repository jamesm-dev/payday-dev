# Cash Flow Runway

## Pitch

A day-by-day projection of your balance for the next eight weeks, built from scheduled pay, bills and
loan payments, with the low points flagged before you reach them.

## Why it might work

- Stolen from corporate treasury: finance teams keep a rolling 13-week cash-flow forecast for exactly
  this reason. The danger is a dip on one specific day, not the monthly total.
- It shows when moving a loan payment or a large purchase is safe, and when it is not.

## Why it might not

- Day-to-day spending is noisy, so the projection needs an estimate per day that can be wrong in
  either direction.
- On a phone it competes with [[Safe To Spend Number]]; one may make the other redundant.

> [!warning] Riskiest assumption
> That scheduled items dominate your cash flow. If day-to-day spending is most of the money, the line
> is mostly guesswork.

## What would raise confidence

- [ ] From the last two months of bank statements, work out what share of money out was scheduled
  (bills, loans) and what share was day-to-day.
